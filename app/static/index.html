Multi-agent AI

Six agents, one job each

Every message passes through a pipeline of specialised agents. Each hands a structured result to the next, and every step is traced so you can see who decided what, and how long it took.

🎧

Listener

Whisper large-v3

Turns Urdu and Punjabi voice notes into text, primed with your brand names.

🧭

Router

gpt-oss-20b

Decides what the message is: a new order, a payment (“bees hazar bhej diye”) or a balance question.

📦

Extraction

Qwen 3.8 → gpt-oss-120b

Pulls out items, quantities, payment and shop, and runs in parallel with the Router to save time.

🗂️

Catalogue

RapidFuzz + price list

Checks every product against your catalogue and prices it from your list. Models never set prices.

✅

Verifier

gpt-oss-20b

A second model re-reads what was said and flags missed items, wrong quantities and ignored corrections.

💳

Credit

Khata ledger rules

Checks each shop's khata against its credit limit and warns before an order goes over it.

Architecture

Agents, a human in the loop, and no single point of failure

Each message flows through the agent pipeline below. If an AI model is busy, the next one takes over, and rule-based fallbacks keep orders moving even with no AI at all.

Specialised agentsEach agent has one job and its own model: Whisper listens, gpt-oss-20b routes and verifies, Qwen and gpt-oss-120b extract.
AI understands, code decidesPrices, totals and balances always come from the database — the model can't invent a number.
Designed not to breakCached samples, a model fallback chain and an offline parser keep orders flowing when an API is busy.
