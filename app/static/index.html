Why distributors use it

Built around how Pakistani trade already works

No new app for shopkeepers to learn. They keep sending voice notes; your team stops typing them out.

Understands real voice notes

Urdu, Punjabi, Roman Urdu and English mixed together — including “dedh carton”, “darjan” and “nahi nahi, teen kar dein”.

Matches your catalogue

Every item is checked against your products and price list. The AI never invents a price; anything uncertain asks “Did you mean…?”.

How it works

From voice note to confirmed order in four steps

The AI does the listening and typing. A person stays in control of every order.

Record or forward

AI understands it

Whisper turns speech into text; an open LLM extracts items, quantities, payment and shop.

Review in one tap

Prices come from your catalogue. Uncertain items are highlighted with one-tap suggestions.

Confirm and reply

The order is saved, the khata updated, and an Urdu reply is ready to send — text or voice.

Architecture

Open models, a human in the loop, and no single point of failure

Each request flows through the pipeline below. If the AI service is rate-limited, a rule-based parser takes over so orders never stop.

Open-source modelsWhisper large-v3 for speech; Qwen 3.8 and gpt-oss for extraction, tried in order across separate rate limits.
AI understands, code decidesPrices, totals and balances always come from the database — the model can't invent a number.
Designed not to breakCached samples, a model fallback chain and an offline parser keep orders flowing when an API is busy.
